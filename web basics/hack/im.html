Information Of Immigration

Family sponsorship: 64% of green card recipients in 2023 were immediate relatives of U.S. citizens or other family-sponsored immigrants. Employer sponsorship: 17% of green card recipients in 2023 were sponsored by their employer or self-petitioned. Humanitarian protection: A pathway for immigrants seeking temporary or permanent residence. Diversity Visa lottery: A pathway for nationals of countries with low levels of immigration to the United States. Green-card lottery: A lesser-used pathway for immigrants with sought-after skills.
